v0.2.315 bustingDrs [fix > slash pick keeps next command]

slash commands:
Picking a slash command from the menu while the cursor sits right in front of another command, such as typing /x before /command, now inserts the picked command to the left with a space: /picked /command. Before this, the command to the right was deleted and replaced. Editing in the middle of a command word, such as /com|mand, still replaces the whole word.

Under the hood: the end of the replaced word now stops at the next "/" as well as at whitespace, the same rule the menu already used to find where the word starts; both rules now live in prompt/slash.ts. Checks: tui slash tests pass with a new case for this bug; tui typecheck clean. Not run: a live check in the real TUI prompt.
