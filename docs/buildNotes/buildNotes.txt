pasting:
Pressing Enter right after pasting an image with Ctrl+V now sends the image together with your text. Before, the text went out alone and the image landed in the next prompt. The same applies to pasting a file path that becomes an attachment, and to Alt+Enter queueing.

Typing, pressing Ctrl+V and then Enter very quickly now sends the prompt with the image even when Ctrl is still held as Enter goes down. That key used to arrive as Ctrl+Enter and add a newline instead of sending. Ctrl+Enter still adds a newline normally; only within a moment of a Ctrl+V paste does it send.

Under the hood: prompt pastes run through trackPaste, and submit and queuePrompt wait for any paste still reading before they read the draft. For 300 ms after Ctrl+V, or until the paste lands if that is later, a targeted ctrl+return binding runs prompt.submit and the Home "send and stay" binding is paused. Cause found with a temporary raw-input log (since removed): Enter came in as \x1b[13;5u 87 ms after Ctrl+V. Checks run: promptPasteSubmit.test.ts (Enter and Ctrl+Enter during a slow image read, 2 pass; each case fails with its fix removed); tui typecheck clean. Not checked live in the TUI after the last fix.
