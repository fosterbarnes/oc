v0.2.202 usuallyFellow

agent-push:
The new /agentPushAutoDelete setting is off by default. When enabled, sessions marked for auto-delete after a successful agent-push are deleted once you leave them. Completed pushed sessions left by closing oc are cleaned up when oc next starts with the setting enabled.

Auto-delete keeps the current session, pinned sessions, running sessions, and sessions open in another tab. Prompting a session again clears its completion state and keeps it. Forks do not inherit the auto-delete marker.

Under the hood: Added the verified-push autoDelete parameter to session_complete, shared completed-session paging with the cleanup dialog, and added cross-tab ledger checks before deletion. The targeted completion and fork tests and prePush checks passed.
