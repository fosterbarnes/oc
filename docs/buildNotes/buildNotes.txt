claude accounts:
Switching the Claude account from the palette, globally or for one session, no longer cuts off work that is running. A chat that is mid-turn, or that has background jobs running (background shell commands, agents, Monitor watches), finishes that work on its current account and moves to the new one on its next message. New chats and subagents use the new account right away.

The sidebar marks the account a chat is actually running on, and flips to the new one once the chat's work ends. The switch dialogs now say the change applies after running work finishes.

Under the hood: the language model keeps a session's account while its Claude process has a turn in flight or running CLI tasks, tracked from the CLI's stream-json task_started / task_notification / task_updated lines, and skips the account retire in that case. Usage-limit failover is unchanged. The account pool state reports the running account as current for a session. Claude's tool note and the wiki now say only a failover, restart or /reload ends background jobs.
