favorite categories:
The model list aligns category headings and models with Favorites, and hides the extra Favorites heading when every favorite belongs to a category. Categories support theme colors and custom hex colors that persist through renaming.

The category picker names the model being edited and shows category counts and color swatches. Searching for a new name offers a Create row that creates and assigns the category in one step. Rename, color, reorder, and press-again delete actions are available directly in the picker. Moving a favorite past a category edge with Shift+Up or Shift+Down moves it into the neighboring category.

Under the hood: Category colors are validated and stored with model preferences. Shared dialog rendering accounts for hidden category headings, and favorite ordering handles cross-category moves, including empty groups. Updated keybind documentation and fork notes. Changed TUI context tests passed. Pre-push validation passed.
