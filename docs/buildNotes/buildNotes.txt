scheduled prompts:
Prompts can be scheduled for this session, a fork, or a new session using a segmented clock with seconds. Due prompts retain ordering and recover safely across process restarts; failed scheduled sends return to the paused queue. Queue and schedule actions are available from the prompt and command palette.
