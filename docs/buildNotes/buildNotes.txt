v0.2.268 sirHoedown

dialogs, prompt, session view, sidebar:
Lists keep your selection when they refresh and show "no matches" separately from "empty". The MCP dialog toggles several servers at once and shows each server's error. alt+return now only queues a prompt; use shift+return, ctrl+return, or ctrl+j for a newline. /editor tells you to set $VISUAL or $EDITOR instead of doing nothing, and the prompt fits narrow terminals better. Diffs show each hunk separately, very large new files open in chunks, shell output shows the newest lines, and subagent permission prompts and questions appear in the parent session. The revert banner says "messages" for more than one, and copy/export include the whole transcript. Session cost now includes what its subagents spent, toasts sit in the top-right of the whole window, and the system theme no longer slows startup.
