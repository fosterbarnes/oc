v0.2.289 homiesElection [feat. > Claude failover can ask which account]

claude accounts:
A new palette entry, "Claude account failover", chooses what happens when a Claude turn hits an account's usage limit. Auto, the default, works as before: the turn continues on the account with the most 5-hour usage left. Ask shows a question inside the running turn that lists your other usable accounts, best first with their 5-hour usage, plus Stop, and the turn continues on the account you pick. The entry shows when two or more accounts are configured.

Stop, dismissing the question, or pressing Esc ends the turn on the limit and keeps the conversation, so auto-resume picks it back up after the reset if it is on. Subagents and agents that cannot ask questions keep switching automatically. In Auto, a chat that is not pinned to an account now always moves to the best-ranked account, even when another chat already moved the global account elsewhere.

Under the hood: the setting is oc.json claudeFailover (auto or ask), read fresh at each failover so it needs no config reload. accounts.ts splits failoverAccount into failoverCandidates (ranked, no side effects) and applyFailoverChoice; turnStream asks through oc's question tool in-stream (new failoverQuestion.ts) and keeps the form out of Claude's history via the account_failover_ id prefix. The host tool executor now carries its tool names. FORK.md documents Ask mode. Checks: bun test accountPool + failoverQuestion (25/25), tsgo typecheck in opencode and tui. The Ask flow in a live turn is not covered by tests yet. The push runs prePush.
