v0.2.340 valveProstate [fix > Mac revive window; patch > no-access note]

revive:
On the Mac in WezTerm, /revive now reopens the window in front of other apps, at the position and size it had before. Before, it opened behind the active app at WezTerm's default spot. A full-screen window comes back full screen when your wezterm.lua gui-startup handles OC_REVIVE_FULLSCREEN; the wiki's Launching page has the snippet.

Claude Code:
When your Claude account can't use the chosen model (for example Claude Mythos 5.1, which is invite-only), the chat now names the account and tells you to pick Claude Fable 5.1. The turn shows as failed instead of as a normal reply.

Under the hood: new core/util/weztermWindow.ts records the window before the TUI exits (bounds from the macOS window list by the GUI pid, cols/rows from wezterm cli list) and treats unreadable output as no placement. reopenCommand on macOS uses open -n -b com.github.wez.wezterm with --config initial_cols/rows before start and --position in pixels, and awaitLaunch now waits for open to exit. The model-access note comes from the CLI's model_not_found error (isModelNotFound, formatModelAccessNote, catalogTwin). Checks: a probe ran the real reopen command and the window landed at the exact spot, frontmost, and full screen when asked; a live /revive came back at the measured place; revive.test.ts 17 pass; weztermWindow tests 2 pass; cliEvents and configModels 22 pass; the new fake-model scenario passes; typecheck clean in core, opencode, and tui. Known: 3 macOS fakeModel failures that also fail on clean main.
