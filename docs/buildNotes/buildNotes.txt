subagent cards:
While a subagent runs, its line in the chat becomes a small box showing its latest tool calls, thinking, and replies, newest at the bottom. The box header shows the agent, task, model, and how full its context is; click it to open the subagent. When the subagent finishes, the box shrinks back to the usual one-line summary. Subagent live rows in the oc palette (/subagentRows) cycles the box height between off, 3, 4, and 6 rows; it starts at 4. Claude Code's own built-in subagents get no box. Parallel subagents stack their boxes.

prompt bar:
The bar under the prompt now shows each subagent's context use, such as "Explore 45% . General 12%", next to the chat's own tokens and cost. Up to three subagents show, newest first, with +N for the rest. Show/Hide subagent usage in the oc palette (/subagentUsage) turns this on or off; it starts on. The ctrl+p "commands" hint now sits at the far right, after ctrl+o "oc commands".

Under the hood: new subagentFeed helper builds the card rows from the child session's live messages and parts (no server change), and the card reuses the transcript's BlockTool inside the Task renderer. New contextUsage helper replaces the duplicated context math in the prompt bar and subagent footer, and subagentLabel moved into util/session.ts. Checks run: tui typecheck clean; subagentFeed tests 3 pass; contextUsage tests 4 pass. Not checked live in the TUI.
