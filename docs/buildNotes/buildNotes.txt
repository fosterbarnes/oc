commit titles:
Agent-pushed commit titles now end with a label and a very short summary in brackets, for example [fix | OpenAI refusal text; deps. | Bun 1.4.2]. The labels are sync, fix, feat., patch, perf., deps., scripts, cleanup, docs, and misc. A title can carry at most two segments, and misc. only on its own. AGENTS.md explains how to pick a label, how to word the summary, and when misc. is and is not allowed. The agent push refuses a title that breaks the format before the slow checks run.

Under the hood: new $commitLabels and assertCommitSubject in .scripts/scriptHelper.ps1; agentPush.ps1 validates line 1 with it, and -Context and -SetTag print the bracket template and the label list. New .scripts/tests/commitSubject.tests.ps1. AGENTS.md's stale upstream conventional-commit section now points to the buildNotes.txt rules; SCRIPTS.md agentPush row updated. Checks already recorded in buildNotes.md: commitSubject.tests.ps1 passed (3 good, 14 bad); agentPush.ps1 -Context printed the template and labels. The push runs prePush.
