Claude mid-turn messages:
When Claude reads a message you sent while it was working, the reply now splits there: what it did before stays above your message and the rest of the turn continues below it, as with other models. The message keeps its "read mid-turn" label, and the reply footer time still counts the whole turn. Messages sent during a slash command now join that turn at its next tool step instead of stopping it, and the command's rules stay on until the turn ends. Background subagent and shell notices reach Claude mid-turn instead of interrupting it, and a wake after a background job no longer restarts Claude's process.

Subagents:
A subagent that errors after writing its final reply passes that reply to the parent with the error.
