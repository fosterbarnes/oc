v0.2.184 blamFan

session view:
When an edit, write, patch or other titled tool block is taller than the screen, its title now stays pinned on the top row while you scroll through it, so you always know which file a long diff belongs to. The pinned title goes away once the block's last line scrolls past. Clicking it jumps back to the top of the block, and the mouse wheel still scrolls while the pointer is over it.

Under the hood: tool blocks register with the session view, which picks the pinned title after each scroll or size change. FORK.md and the build notes describe the feature. Also includes pending bun.lock and opencode package.json changes. TUI typecheck passed, and the pinned title was confirmed working in the installed TUI. prePush passed.
