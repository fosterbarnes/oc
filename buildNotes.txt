bgrun tool:
New "bgRun tool" toggle in the oc section of the command palette (or `/bgRun`) turns the background-job tool on or off for agents. Off hides bgRun, bgStatus, and bgKill from agents entirely.

Under the hood: the toggle writes allow/deny for bgRun, bgStatus, and bgKill together through the global config update, keeping comments in opencode.jsonc. The background-marker experiment from the unpushed v0.2.103 entries was abandoned and reverted, so markers behave as in v0.2.102. This push's focused check passed: typecheck clean in tui.
