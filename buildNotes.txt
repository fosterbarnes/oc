sidebar position:
The oc section of the command palette has a new "Sidebar position (WIP)" setting (or /sidebarPosition) with right, top, left, and bottom choices, and the choice is remembered between sessions. At the top the sidebar becomes a horizontal bar above the messages; at the bottom the same bar sits below the messages and prompt. On the left the full sidebar sits beside the messages on wide terminals and opens as a panel on the left side on narrow ones. The top and bottom bars also show on narrow terminals where the right-hand sidebar stays hidden.

Under the hood: the session view reads one saved sidebar_position key and places the sidebar through a single leading memo plus one trailing switch. The horizontal bar wraps its sections, caps at 12 rows with no drawn scrollbar, and keeps footer spacing consistent. This push's focused check passed: typecheck clean in tui. Live visual confirmation came from user screenshots.
