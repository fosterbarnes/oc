claude code provider:
When a chat's Claude conversation is lost and you choose "Start fresh", the new Claude no longer starts blind. Its first message now carries your previous message and the end of its last reply, so follow-ups like "do option b" work, plus instructions to read older turns of the chat through session_read when it needs more. It is told to treat the old transcript as background and not to redo earlier work. The lost-conversation question and notice now describe this instead of saying Claude remembers nothing.

Under the hood: the catch-up block is built in message-builder.ts (resumeBlock) and sent only on the first message of the new conversation. Excerpts are capped at 1500 and 4000 characters and keep their ends. The session_read pointer is included only when the chat has a real session id and session_read is in proxyOpencodeTools. The message builder tests pass 19/19 with 4 new cases, and typecheck is clean. Not yet checked live.
