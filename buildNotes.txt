v0.2.100

text selection:
Double-click selects a word, triple-click selects a logical line including its wrapped rows, and dragging after those clicks extends by whole words or lines. Copy-on-release and Ctrl+C both keep the highlight visible. Escape clears selection before closing a dialog or interrupting a session. Triple-click selects lines, not linguistic sentences.

message and tool controls:
User messages have a visible Actions control. Tool expand/collapse and file-open actions use separate controls or headers, and inline tool actions use their icon. Text bodies stay selectable without the first click opening a dialog or changing the layout. Explicit links and loaded paths remain action controls.

Under the hood: matched OpenTUI packages move to 0.5.14 for native selection gestures, with SolidJS 1.9.12 and a version-specific cleanup patch. Root and modal selection use shared copy handling, and clipboard writes finish in gesture order.
