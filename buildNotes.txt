v0.2.135

message actions:
Clicking a sent message opens its Actions menu again, including undo. Clickable messages no longer select text on double-click.

tool rows:
Clicking anywhere on an actionable tool or subagent row runs its action, even when another part of the conversation has a highlight. Clickable rows and the redo banner are not text-selectable; ordinary text keeps double-click selection.

Under the hood: moved tool click handling onto the whole row, removed the lingering-selection guard, and added selectable control to spinner text. Updated the fork definition and renderer selection checks. Targeted textSelection tests and pre-push checks passed.
